# Sensory & Cognitive Distortion Simulator

An interactive illustration of sensory overload in a classroom. Visitors choose a grade, answer 10 timed math questions on a chalkboard while glare, background noise, racing thoughts, and restlessness build up, then see what actually helps autistic children and children with ADHD at school.

Live site: https://mohdashaar786-alt.github.io/New-ADHD-Stimulation-/

This is an awareness tool, not a diagnostic test or medical advice. Sources are listed at the bottom of the page.

## Safety

- The glare effect is slow and never flashes.
- Sound is off until the visitor turns it on.
- Visitors who choose "reduce motion" on their device see no shaking or moving effects.
